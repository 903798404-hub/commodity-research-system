Statistics for 12 Major Oilmeals

The Commodity Sections for Oilseeds, Oils and Oilmeals provide the supply and demand developments as well as stocks, generally with breakdown by major countries. The forecasts cover the season 2026/27 for crop production and area as well as world supply & demand of the major oilseeds and products. If not indicated otherwise, the time periods in most tables are the 5 seasons until 2025/26 and the 3 calendar years ending 2025.  A quarterly breakdown is shown in the respective Summary Tables of each commodity.    The section 'Other Commodities' provides world production statistics for red meat.   Please refer to the General Notes at the end of the index for further explanations.
