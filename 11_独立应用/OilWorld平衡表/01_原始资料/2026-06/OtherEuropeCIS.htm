Statistics for Other Europe / C.I.S.

Most statistics refer to the 5 seasons until 2025/26 and the 3 calendar years ending 2025 as well as the split April/Sept and Oct/March. Crop production is shown until 2026/27. Other time references, e.g. quarterly split, are indicated in the title. The tables provide Oilseed Area, Yields & Production as well as Oilseed Balances, Crushings, Trade plus the Supply and Demand Balances of Oils/Fats and Oilmeals. Further information on time references, definitions, etc. is given in the General Notes.
